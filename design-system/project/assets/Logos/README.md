# Logos

The Bidulgi Engine mark: a standing pigeon with human lips and human feet, drawn as one flat silhouette.

- `icon-d-white.svg`: single ink white (#ffffff). The default mark on dark surfaces (`ground`, `win`, `panel`); 18px in the menubar beside the `brand` wordmark.
- `icon-d-black.svg`: single ink near-black (#12161c). For light backgrounds such as documents and print.
- `icon-d-tile.svg`: the white mark on a dark rounded tile. App icon, taskbar, favicon.
- Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's width. Do not recolor, outline, add gradients or rotate the mark.
